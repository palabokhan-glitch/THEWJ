Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your inquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, send newsletter updates you have subscribed to, and improve our services. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Confidentiality

Any information shared with us through our contact form is treated as confidential. Please note, however, that submitting an inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship, and information shared prior to engagement may not be protected by attorney-client privilege.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.
