Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with WJ Law Firm.

Home → FAQ

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. All new clients receive a free initial consultation to discuss their case and determine the best path forward.

What are your fees?

Our fee structures vary by practice area and case complexity. We offer flat fees, hourly billing, and contingency arrangements depending on your matter — details are provided during your consultation.

How long will my case take?

Timelines vary widely depending on the type and complexity of your case. Your attorney will provide a realistic estimate after reviewing the details.

Do you handle cases outside your listed practice areas?

We have a trusted network of referral partners for matters outside our core practice areas and are happy to make an introduction.

Where is your office located?

Our office is located at A-21, First Floor, Swiss Plaza, Jinnah Road, Quetta. See our Contact page for full address and directions.
