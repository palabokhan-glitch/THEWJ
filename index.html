Welcome to WJ Law Firm

Trusted Counsel, Led by Barrister Wasil Jan

WJ Law Firm is a Quetta-based legal practice led by Managing Partner Barrister Wasil Jan, a constitutional law expert and an independent Member of the Advisory Council of the Balochistan Revenue Authority, with extensive experience in public-private partnerships, transaction advisory, tax law, regulatory compliance, intellectual property, and public procurement. The firm represents corporations, regulatory bodies, public-sector institutions, and individuals across Balochistan and beyond, providing strategic, commercially focused, and results-driven legal counsel tailored to the unique needs of every client and matter.
