Overview

Comprehensive Legal Representation

WJ Law Firm represents corporations, government institutions, public bodies, businesses, and individuals before the High Courts, statutory tribunals, regulatory authorities, and other judicial forums across Pakistan. Led by Barrister Wasil Jan, our practice spans constitutional and administrative law, corporate and commercial matters, regulatory and tax law, criminal defense, and civil litigation.

Whatever forum your matter falls under, our attorneys bring the same standard of preparation, strategic judgment, and client-focused advocacy.
