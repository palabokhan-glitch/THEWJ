Our Story

Barrister Wasil Jan, Managing Partner

WJ Law Firm is an independent and well-established law firm based in Quetta, spearheaded by Barrister Wasil Jan. We are a full-service law firm serving international and domestic clients. With a team of highly skilled and experienced attorneys, we are committed to serving our clients with integrity, professionalism, and a results-driven approach.

Wasil's journey began with an LLB (Hons) from the University of London, International Programme in 2017, followed by a role as an Associate at AB Associates, where he first delved into litigation. He then pursued an LLM from Queen Mary, University of London, focused on Energy and Natural Resources. Returning to Quetta in 2019, he established WJ Law Firm in August 2021. His dedication culminated in his formal Call to the Bar of England and Wales by The Honorable Society of Lincoln's Inn in March 2023. His legal expertise spans a wide spectrum, with a particular focus on Constitutional Law, Service Law, and Criminal Law.
