Our Story

Barrister Wasil Jan, Managing Partner

Barrister Wasil Jan is a distinguished legal professional and Managing Partner at WJ Law Firm in Quetta, Pakistan. With a strong academic foundation, including an LLM in Energy and Natural Resources from Queen Mary, University of London, and a Call to the Bar from The Honorable Society of Lincoln's Inn, Wasil specializes in public-private partnerships (PPP), transaction advisory, and tax laws.

Since 2021, he has successfully managed a high-volume caseload, representing corporations, regulatory bodies, and individuals in complex legal matters. His notable clients include the Balochistan Revenue Authority, Balochistan Public Private Partnership Authority, Sui Southern Gas Company, Quetta Electricity Supply Company Limited, and the National Bank of Pakistan. Wasil serves as an independent member of the Advisory Council for the Balochistan Revenue Authority, providing strategic guidance on tax laws, compliance frameworks, and revenue generation strategies.
